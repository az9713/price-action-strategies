# Numerical rulebook — version 0.25

Status: implementation contract under active verification. This rulebook specifies our deterministic equity adaptation of the source video. It does not claim access to the host's private code or numerical reproduction of his results. The public configuration schema is generated from `backend/lab/config.py`; unknown fields and invalid combinations are errors.

## Time and prices

Input timestamps are UTC **minute opens**, not candle closes. Naive imported timestamps require an explicit timezone. Regular XNYS sessions, including holidays/early closes and DST, define the execution calendar. Missing expected minutes block an ordinary research run; no price is forward-filled. Identical duplicate bars can be removed with a recorded count; conflicting duplicates are errors. Minute OHLCV must be finite, positive-price and internally consistent.

The six setup charts are 1m, 5m, 15m, 1h, 4h and daily. Aggregate from each session's open without crossing sessions; keep the shorter last bar. A daily bar spans that actual session, not a fixed 390 minutes on an early close. A candle is usable only at its scheduled close and only when its expected minute rows are present. Daily setups trigger on hourly candles; other triggers use the setup chart or one/two adjacent steps lower. Lower-chart structure confirmation must actually select a lower trigger chart.

Splits use **forward causal normalization**: multiply raw prices by the cumulative new-share/old-share ratio effective by that minute's session. The factor is never applied before the split becomes effective. Thus a 2:1 split changes raw 100 to raw 50 while signal prices remain continuous at 100. Candidate levels/stops are stored in this normalized numeraire and divided by the current factor at execution. Open positions, stops and targets transform at a split. Fractional shares caused by corporate actions are retained until exit; new orders use whole shares. Cash-in-lieu/merger treatment beyond this contract is not silently invented.

ATR is Wilder's 14-bar true-range average using **prior completed bars only**. The first usable ATR is the mean of true ranges 0–13 at bar 14, followed by the Wilder recursion. Overnight gaps count in true range; split normalization prevents mechanical split gaps. No synthetic volume is inserted.

## Confirmed structure and levels

A strength-k pivot is the unique strict high/low in a 2k+1-bar window and becomes known k bars later. Equal extrema do not qualify. Strength choices are 2, 3, 5 and 8. Trend is first initialized when the last two confirmed highs and lows both rise or both fall. It persists until an opposite qualifying structure break; mixed pivot changes alone do not reset an established trend. A first close strictly through the latest confirmed high/low is a structure event, once per pivot. With-trend events are BOS; against-trend events are CHoCH and reverse structural state. Wick penetration or exact equality is not a close break.

Confirmed pivots join same-side level clusters within 0.1, 0.25 or 0.5 current prior ATR. Choose the nearest cluster, breaking ties by oldest creation. Its center is the equal-weight mean of member pivot prices **as known now**; historical signals are never revised. Level lifetime counts setup bars from creation. A touch overlaps the tolerance band; adjacent overlapping bars count as one visit. Minimum touches is 1–4. Line versus zone changes breakout/bounce edges by the configured tolerance band. Expired/broken levels cannot provide fresh bounce signals.

Touch visits count from confirmed level creation; pre-confirmation pivot bars are not retrospectively counted as visits. A level emits only its first qualifying bounce in a visit. Qualifying visits are consumed even when a competing setup wins that bar or the bar is rejected as ambiguous. Separated visits may create later candidates. A zone bounce uses its far band edge as its candidate price while the close-back test references the cluster center; a zone breakout requires a close through its outward band edge.

When multiple candidates of one family qualify in a completed bar, choose the newest: level creation for bounce/breakout, break bar for flips, penetration bar for sweeps, second confirmed anchor for lines, impulse completion for demand/supply and gap completion for gaps. Equally new opposite-direction candidates are ambiguous and produce no setup; the direction filter is applied afterward. Equally new same-direction candidates use the price nearest the completed close. A gap-confluence filter checks existence in the candidate's direction; another opposite gap cannot erase that state. Requiring a fair-value gap to supply its own additional gap confluence is redundant and is rejected as a nonapplicable parameter.

## Ten distinct setup families

| Family | Precise candidate event |
|---|---|
| Level bounce | From the appropriate side, candle touches a confirmed unbroken level and closes back on that side; previous close must also have been on that side. |
| Breakout | First completed close crosses the qualifying line/zone edge relative to previous close, once per level. |
| Flip zone | A level was broken, then a later candle returns into its band and closes on the new side before `retest_window` expires. Consume that first retest, including an unsuccessful one. |
| Trend pullback | Established trend, price overlaps the last confirmed with-trend swing level, reversal body agrees with trend, and retreat from the last ten-bar extreme lies within configured ATR depth bounds. First qualifying pullback per structural leg; a continuation break resets that side's eligibility. |
| Trend line | Project the line through the latest two confirmed lows (rising long) or highs (falling short). Candle overlaps its band and closes on the trend side. Later pivots only affect later projections. |
| BOS continuation | First confirmed swing close-break agrees with the existing structural trend. |
| CHoCH reversal | First confirmed swing close-break opposes the existing structural trend. Optional sweep prerequisite needs a recorded same-direction reclaim in the recent configured window; default requires no sweep. |
| Sweep/reclaim | Price penetrates the latest known opposing swing by more than configured ATR depth and closes back across that swing within `reclaim_bars`. The signal is known at the reclaim close, never at the earlier sweep. |
| Supply/demand | A run of `impulse_bars` directional bodies spans at least `impulse_atr` prior ATR. The preceding candle's high/low defines the base zone. Candidate is its first later overlapping return, unless its far boundary was already closed through or it expired. |
| Fair-value gap | After three closed candles, low[2] > high[0] defines a bullish gap, or high[2] < low[0] a bearish gap. Candidate is its first later return before expiry/invalidation. A gap-middle-candle stop references the appropriate extreme of the middle candle. Equality is not a gap. |

These definitions resolve ambiguities absent from the source; changes require a versioned decision and regression tests. Six named host presets remain unreproduced because their full configurations are not supplied. Registry labels must call these actual separate detectors; a menu alias is not another implemented family.

## Confirmation and filters

No-confirmation candidates are known at the setup close. Other modes inspect completed trigger candles at or after that instant for at most `confirmation_window` candles. Confirmation can be a close back across the level, a rejection wick occupying at least half the full candle range, a directional engulfing body covering the preceding body, either wick/engulfing, or a same-direction lower-chart BOS. Candidate and confirmation timestamps remain separate.

Direction is long, short or both. Core-session signals use the actual regular calendar. Open-session signals must become known from 09:30 inclusive to 11:00 exclusive ET. A trend filter compares the last available completed setup, next-higher or daily structural state. The choppiness filter rejects ten-bar efficiency ratio below 0.3, with zero total movement assigned zero efficiency. A 3-/5-session run filter compares current known close with the corresponding prior trading-session close and rejects a same-direction run. News skip/half-size requires a supplied, versioned calendar; absent news information is an error. Gap confluence requires a known same-direction unfilled gap. Fresh flip-zone retests require the breakout in the preceding setup bar; other eligible break families already emit first breaks.

News and run filters use the New York date when the signal becomes known at confirmation, including an overnight confirmation. They do not use the earlier setup date or a later execution date. A run filter excludes a daily candle from that same confirmation date before selecting the third/fifth prior trading-session close; only completed daily candles available by confirmation can contribute. This corrects the setup-date behavior exposed by the version0.10 overnight counterexamples. The calendar input/acquisition/versioning workflow remains a separate requirement from these selector-level date rules.

The public schema distinguishes parameters used by the chosen setup from shared parameters; setup-specific controls must be presented with applicability. No unsupported arbitrary Python or strategy code executes through the app.

## Orders and costs

Default entry is the next eligible minute open after a signal becomes available. Warm-up builds state only and cannot submit entries before the selected evaluation window. Same-close is an explicit alternate synthetic fill assumption; it cannot earn profit from earlier movement in that candle. Limit entry is at the signal level, with at least one tick of trade-through and no favorable gap price improvement; expiry counts elapsed trigger-chart minutes. A limit entry's ambiguous first minute can stop out but receives no target credit because intraminute ordering is unknown.

On a discrete grid, a buy limit is rounded **down** from its maximum acceptable level, and a sell limit **up** from its minimum acceptable level. Require one full tick through that submitted limit. Exact one-tick target/limit thresholds and prior-volume participation are compared as decimal values, not binary floating additions/floors. Limit expiry uses elapsed wall-clock minutes, including overnight/weekend gaps, multiplied by the selected trigger-chart minute length; timeout exits separately count completed trading candles.

Stops reference the known swing, level, candidate wick or (gap family only) middle candle, plus 0/0.1/0.25 prior ATR. Round outward to the equity tick grid. Nonpositive post-gap risk rejects the order. Targets are configured R multiples or a valid known opposing swing. Limits/targets need one-tick trade-through; exact touch is insufficient. Hard stops trigger on touch and fill one tick adversely plus configured market costs. At an opening gap past the stop, use the opening price with those adverse costs, not the old stop. Stop wins any unresolved same-minute stop/target conflict.

Close-based stops inspect only completed trigger-chart closes strictly beyond the stop. Structure exits use a completed opposite CHoCH. Optional timeouts count completed setup candles since entry, not wall-clock hours over weekends. Session-flat uses the actual session close and is rejected for 4h/daily setups. End-of-window positions liquidate at the last supplied eligible close; this artificial boundary exit is identified and is not a future-information assertion about earlier signals.

Market entry/exit prices include configured slippage and half spread in basis points, rounded adversely to the tick grid. Decimal input text defines tick boundaries, avoiding binary floating-point promotion by a whole tick. Commission is max(per-share charge, minimum commission), plus fixed order fee; a separate per-dollar sell fee applies only to sells. Equity defaults are declared scenarios, not a claim about the user's broker schedule. Targets/limit entries execute at their limits without market slippage, while commissions still apply. A target must remain strictly positive after tick rounding. A modeled nonpositive market execution is an explicit unsupported-price/grid error, never a negative-price fill. The source's futures-specific $4/one-tick convention is not an equity default.

If a newly fillable limit enters at a session/minute open already beyond its hard stop, retain the conservative no-improvement limit entry and immediately execute its stop at that opening market price with adverse costs. Both events occur at that open. Do not pretend the old stop was available. This resolves an omitted boundary in version 0.1; it is a conservative bar-model adaptation, not a guarantee about actual broker fills.

## Capital, short positions and distributions

One position per symbol/configuration is allowed. Different configurations can share the same portfolio budget. Size is the **largest feasible whole-share quantity** satisfying risk, per-symbol/aggregate gross exposure, cash including fees, and prior-minute volume participation. Same-close entries may use that just-completed minute's volume. Modeled hard-stop slippage and both commissions are reserved in planned risk; real gaps can exceed that budget. Select simultaneously eligible pending orders globally by signal availability, symbol, configuration identity, then signal identity; an older pending order does not lose priority merely because another instrument sorts earlier. At an opening fill, risk uses current opening marks. At a synthetic close fill, all instruments' just-completed closing marks are applied first. Short proceeds do not increase the gross exposure budget.

Daily equity is cash plus signed marked position value plus unpaid distribution receivables/liabilities. Short financing accrues over elapsed calendar seconds / (365×86400). Each trading minute uses the opening notional known at its start; financing is charged through that minute's close before exit decisions and daily equity. Between observations, use the last known closing notional, including weekends. An entry at a synthetic close owes no financing for the preceding minute. Thus daily equity includes its own last-minute charge rather than deferring it until the next session. Unavailable borrow rejects shorts. Recalls and historical availability require an explicit scenario/input and must not be inferred from price data. A distribution entitles positions carried into its ex-session, crediting longs/debiting shorts; it changes receivable equity then pays cash on the supplied payable date (or the next available observation). If no payable date is supplied, ex-date settlement is explicitly the selected assumption. Splits effective on the same session are applied before per-current-share distribution entitlement.

Every cash event records before/change/after amounts and a trade ID. Net trade profit includes price profit, distributions, borrowing and both execution fees. Mark-to-market equity and independent ledger identities, rather than exit-day profit alone, are the primary risk basis. Trading logic is not a profit promise.

Cash/financing retain fractional cents until display; no silent cent rounding is introduced into the ledger. Independent reference comparisons require exact decisions/order/timestamps, price and quantity error at most 1e-9, and monetary error at most 1e-7 dollars on the bounded fixture domain. Net R divides net profit by filled-entry-to-stop price distance times shares; that denominator excludes fees, borrowing and the extra modeled adverse stop tick. Versions 0.2–0.3 fix sizing, priority, financing timing, quote boundaries and tick-permitted limits found by the independent fixtures; older saved engine outputs remain historical outputs and are not relabeled as current verification.

## Aggregate entry participation

The whole-share entry participation budget belongs to an instrument and its reference completed minute, not to a strategy. Across all configurations, total new entry shares using that same reference volume cannot exceed floor(volume × participation). A synthetic close uses the just-completed minute; next-open/limit entries use the latest prior completed minute. The synthetic close and immediately following opening entries therefore share a budget. A genuinely new completed volume sample supplies a new budget. Keep separate budgets per instrument. The candle-close stage precedes the following opening stage at a common timestamp. An immediate gap-stop exit does not restore consumed entry liquidity. Exits retain the declared full conservative bar-fill assumption and do not consume this entry-only cap; actual exit liquidity is not established.

Each accepted entry stores its reference-minute timestamp/volume, whole-share participation limit, and consumed shares before/after the fill in the cash ledger. This permits saved-output inspection of the shared entry cap without reopening protected raw prices.

## Marked metrics and statistical assumptions

Metrics require finite equity/trade P&L and positive finite starting capital. Net profit is final marked equity minus initial capital. Drawdown uses the running daily marked-equity peak including initial capital and can exceed100% after a deficit. Retain all eligible session closes, including no-trade sessions. Daily percentage return is the equity change divided by preceding positive equity; use null when that base is zero or negative. If any equity is nonpositive, percentage-return Sharpe is undefined with an explicit insolvency note; dollar losses, drawdown and total return remain visible. Otherwise use sample standard deviation (ddof1), subtract the declared annual risk-free assumption/252 per observation and annualize by sqrt252. Zero variation and insufficient samples remain undefined.

Deflated Sharpe uses per-observation Sharpe, observation count, unbiased sample skewness and unbiased raw kurtosis (SciPy bias=False), effective independent trials and a supplied cross-trial per-observation Sharpe standard deviation. Neither trial independence nor dispersion is silently inferred from raw configuration count. Invalid/nonfinite assumptions or a nonpositive estimated Sharpe variance yield null. The moments-level entry point reproduces the primary paper's worked examples without fabricating a return path: [Bailey and López de Prado,2014](https://davidhbailey.com/dhbpapers/deflated-sharpe.pdf), pages9–10. Its probability is not a promise of profitability.

The daily-dollar P&L interval uses seeded circular moving blocks, default five sessions and2,000 resamples, with2.5/97.5% linearly interpolated quantiles. Incomplete final blocks are truncated to the original number of sessions; every session remains eligible as a circular start. Resamples2–100000, block length1–100000 and unsigned64-bit integer seed are explicit validated bounds. Batching limits temporary sample arrays without changing the seed sequence. Fewer than two observations yield null. This interval does not correct a selected strategy's search bias.
