## Financial execution

`barrier_capped_elapsed_v1` transports the original entry ATR stop/target distances, centers them on the modeled filled opening, rounds outward on the tick grid, and applies the original hard/close-stop trigger clock subject to the exact elapsed cap. `fixed_elapsed_v1` records barrier encounters while retaining the exact duration unless a mandatory short recall prevents it. The same candidate draws will be used for both policies when the durable set workflow is implemented.

Each trade carries the original whole entry quantity, then authoritative fractional split adjustments. Exogenous diagnostic capital is `max(original capital, entry quantity × modeled fill + entry fee)` for either direction. Capital top-up and entry notional are published; no shared allocation, margin maintenance, cash interest, implicit credit charge or executable aggregate portfolio is inferred. Original participation against the immediately preceding expected completed exchange minute remains binding. Missing or stale prior volume, insufficient capacity and unavailable shorts produce explicit failed candidates; no smaller quantity or replacement draw.

Both implementations retain Decimal monetary amounts, including large-capital fractional cents. Durable storage must preserve canonical decimal strings; it must not silently round a set total before comparison. The engine applies all existing commission fields, spread/slippage/ticks, changing calendar-time borrowing, recalls, splits, dividend entitlements, actual later payments and unpaid receivables. Nonpositive modeled quotes remain explicit computation errors; the future durable worker must retain those failed attempts without grading a successful subset. The kernel does not yet establish durable error retention.

The independent reference was authored first and imports no production numerical code. It uses a chronological open/close event list, while production uses a minute loop, exact rational tick geometry and the existing borrowing/calendar schemas. They share the pinned exchange-calendar dependency, not a claim of independent market-calendar facts. Complete-value parity covers trade, ledger, session equity, rejection, funding, geometry, encounter and final accounting fields. Hand calculations separately verify basic long/short profit, individual fees, weekend/DST borrow and large-capital precision. A stale-liquidity counterexample exposed a shared initial mistake and was corrected in both implementations without loosening tolerances.
