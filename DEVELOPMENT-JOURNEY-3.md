## Capacity and recovery

Early large searches exposed SQLite writer contention and repeated verification of the entire frozen trial plan during individual checkpoint writes. Subsequent fixes reused the original plan projection with fresh validation, read ownership through a covering index, and coordinated committed lease renewals between the worker and heartbeat. A temporary lock had also caused the main progress writer to fail a job. Bounded retries resolved that case while retaining the original lease and renewal deadlines. [Contention reproduction](verification/receipts/20261110T192852203457Z-checkpoint-lock-actual-10000-plan-and-real-heartbeat-contention-before-fix.json) · [Lease regression](verification/receipts/20261110T213704041172Z-main-progress-retry-original-store-trials-runtime-and-all-lease-contracts.json)

That build completed the real-data 1,000-configuration search in **482.77 seconds**, including all 1,000 financial attempts, and verified every saved account. The 10,000-configuration search reached **4,408** committed accounts in **900.52 seconds**, exhausting the 900-second budget. Its process used 800.03 CPU seconds and reached a peak working set of 332,279,808 bytes. After the process exited, the queued continuation was canceled and all 4,408 original files, trial rows and attempt digests were checked. [1,000-configuration result](verification/receipts/20261110T214002786205Z-main-retry-current-real-market-1000-capacity.json) · [10,000-configuration failure](verification/receipts/20261110T214811540835Z-main-retry-current-real-market-10000-capacity.json) · [Original-record preservation](verification/receipts/20261110T220522577334Z-retire-terminal-900s-benchmark-queued-continuation-preserving-4408-originals.json)

A short stack profile identified checkpoint publication and full-plan validation as substantial costs. I chose to publish up to **eight reports and 8 MiB of encoded data** per transaction. Each report keeps its individual computation, attempt, file and digest. The transaction validates the full original plan, checks ownership and cancellation, and commits the batch only after the source and candidate files pass validation. The original single-report path handles oversized reports. [Profile](verification/receipts/20261110T215106991510Z-current-main-retry-market10000-short-readonly-nonblocking-profile-no-locals.json) · [Publication tests](verification/receipts/20261110T221051712970Z-bounded-account-publication-all-originals-rollback-controls-and-encoded-limits.json)

The first financial comparison incorrectly required separate jobs to share an identifier. I corrected it to verify each job’s identity independently and compare every remaining result field and saved report byte. The corrected suite passed nine checks, including five actual process deaths. Committed reports survived recovery. Unfinished reports were recomputed with their attempts recorded, and the recovered results matched uninterrupted runs byte for byte. A Windows verification launcher also returned while its child was running. The child’s process identity was confirmed and a separate monitor attached.
